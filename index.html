About

Hector Kohler is an Assistant Professor (tenure track) at Télécom Paris. He is funded by Hi! Paris to research Reinforcement Learning. He is interested in solving hard (computer) science problems with Reinforcement Learning. Such hard problems include optimization on graphs, and communications in multi-agent networks, for which the DIG team of LTCI is internationally recognized.

Hector Kohler is also developing methodologies to comprehensively evaluate new Reinforcement Learning algorithms and to promote open and reproducible science. Before joining IP Paris, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at École Polytechnique and a visiting scholar at the RLAI lab from the University of Alberta in Canada. Hector Kohler completed his Ph.D. in the Scool team from Inria Lille.
